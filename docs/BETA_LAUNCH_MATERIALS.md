## 🎯 Target Customer Lists

### Local Coffee Shops
- Search "coffee shop near me" on Google Maps
- Look for shops with good reviews but weak online presence
- Focus on independent shops, not chains

### Boutique Retail
- Search "boutique" or "specialty store" in your area
- Look for stores with unique products
- Focus on stores with Instagram presence

### Museums & Galleries
- Search "museum" or "art gallery" in your area
- Look for smaller, independent institutions
- Focus on places that need digital engagement

### Real Estate Offices
- Search "real estate" in your area
- Look for agents with empty commercial properties
- Focus on commercial real estate
