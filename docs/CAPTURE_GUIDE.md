# Google Street View Capture Guide

## How to Capture Perfect 360° Photos for Your Store

### 📱 Method 1: Google Street View App (Recommended)

**Step 1: Download the App**
- **iOS:** https://apps.apple.com/app/google-street-view/id904418768
- **Android:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.street

**Step 2: Capture Your Space**
1. Open the app and tap the **camera icon**
2. Stand in the **center** of your space
3. Hold your phone **at chest height** (about 4-5 feet)
4. Tap the **shutter button** and rotate slowly in a full circle
5. Keep the phone **level** and move at a steady pace
6. The app will automatically stitch the photos together

**Step 3: Export and Upload**
1. Tap the **share icon** on your photo
2. Select **"Export as JPG"** or **"Save to Photos"**
3. Upload to Walk In at https://walk-in-cfa.pages.dev/simple

### 📷 Method 2: 360° Camera (Professional)

**Recommended Cameras:**
- **Insta360 X4** (~$400) — Best quality, easy to use
- **Ricoh Theta Z1** (~$1000) — Professional quality
- **GoPro Max** (~$500) — Rugged, good for action

**Capture Tips:**
1. Place the camera on a tripod in the center of your space
2. Make sure the camera is level
3. Use the camera's app to capture a full 360° photo
4. Export as equirectangular JPG (2:1 aspect ratio)

## 📐 Technical Requirements

**For Best Results:**
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Aspect Ratio:** 2:1 (equirectangular)
- **Resolution:** 4096×2048 or higher
- **File Size:** Under 50MB
- **Projection:** Equirectangular (360°×180°)

**What is Equirectangular?**
- It's a flat representation of a 360° sphere
- The width is exactly 2× the height
- It looks distorted but wraps perfectly around a 3D sphere
- Most 360° cameras and apps export this format automatically

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**"My photo looks distorted"**
- Make sure it's equirectangular format (2:1 aspect ratio)
- Try re-capturing with the Google Street View app
- Check that the horizon is level

**"The 3D view is blurry"**
- Use a higher resolution photo (4096×2048 or higher)
- Make sure the photo is in focus
- Try capturing in better lighting

**"I can't upload my photo"**
- Check the file size (should be under 50MB)
- Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Make sure it's a JPG or PNG file
