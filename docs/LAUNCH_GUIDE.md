# Walk In — Launch Guide

**Your complete step-by-step guide to making your first $1,000 with Walk In.**

---

## 🎯 The Goal

**Make $1,000 in your first month** by signing up 10-20 beta customers at $49.50/month (50% off Pro plan).

---

## 📋 Week 1: Setup & Preparation

### Day 1: Test Your Platform

**Morning (30 min):**
1. Go to https://walk-in-cfa.pages.dev/simple
2. Upload a 360° photo (use Google Street View app)
3. Add 3 products with prices
4. Share the link with yourself
5. Test on mobile and desktop

**Afternoon (30 min):**
1. Go to https://walk-in-cfa.pages.dev/pricing
2. Click "Monthly" on Pro plan
3. Use test card: 4242 4242 4242 4242
4. Verify redirect to owner dashboard
5. Check Stripe dashboard for test payment

**Evening (30 min):**
1. Read `docs/BETA_ONBOARDING.md`
2. Read `docs/BETA_LAUNCH_MATERIALS.md`
3. Prepare your customer list (see below)

### Day 2: Build Your Customer List

**Target: 50 potential customers**

**Where to find them:**
1. **Google Maps** — Search "coffee shop near me", "boutique near me"
2. **Instagram** — Search local business hashtags
3. **Yelp** — Filter by rating (3.5-4.5 stars = good businesses, bad websites)
4. **Your network** — Friends, family, former colleagues
5. **Local business associations** — Chamber of Commerce, BNI groups

**Create a spreadsheet with:**
- Business name
- Owner name
- Email/phone
- Business type
- Website quality (bad = good prospect)
- Notes

### Day 3: Prepare Your Pitch

**Customize the email template in `docs/BETA_LAUNCH_MATERIALS.md`:**

1. **Subject line options:**
   - "Your store, but online and in 3D (beta invite)"
   - "Quick question about [Business Name]'s online presence"
   - "Turn your store into a 24/7 showroom (60 seconds)"

2. **Personalization points:**
   - Mention their specific business
   - Reference something you like about their store
   - Note their weak online presence (if applicable)

3. **Your story:**
   - Why you built this
   - What problem it solves
   - Why you're reaching out to them specifically

### Day 4-5: Set Up Tracking

**Create a simple tracking system:**

**Google Sheets columns:**
- Business Name
- Owner Name
- Email
- Phone
- Business Type
- Outreach Date
- Response Date
- Status (Not contacted / Contacted / Interested / Demo scheduled / Signed up / Paid)
- Notes
- Payment Link Used
- First Store Created (date)
- MRR

**Set up calendar:**
- Block 2 hours every day for outreach
- Block 1 hour for follow-ups
- Block 1 hour for product improvements

### Day 6-7: Soft Launch to Friends

**Test with 3-5 friends or family:**
1. Send them the beta invite email
2. Get their feedback on the pitch
3. Have them test the platform
4. Ask: "Would you pay $49.50/month for this?"
5. Refine based on their feedback

---

## 📧 Week 2: Outreach & Signups

### Day 8-12: Daily Outreach

**Morning routine (2 hours):**
1. **Send 10 emails** from your customer list
2. **Personalize each one** (mention their business name)
3. **Track in your spreadsheet**
4. **Follow up on previous emails** (3-day rule)

**Afternoon routine (1 hour):**
1. **Respond to replies** within 2 hours
2. **Schedule demo calls** for interested prospects
3. **Send payment links** to ready-to-buy customers

**Evening routine (1 hour):**
1. **Update tracking spreadsheet**
2. **Prepare tomorrow's list**
3. **Review and improve your pitch**

### Day 13-14: Follow-up & Close

**Follow-up sequence:**
- **Day 1:** Initial email
- **Day 3:** "Did you see my email about Walk In?"
- **Day 7:** "Last chance for 50% off beta pricing"
- **Day 14:** "Closing beta enrollment this week"

**Closing script:**
"Hi [Name], I'm closing beta enrollment this week. If you're still interested in turning your store into a 24/7 online showroom, now's the time. The 50% discount ends soon. Want me to send you the link?"

## 💰 Week 3: Onboarding & Revenue

### Day 15-17: Onboard First Customers

**When someone pays:**
1. **Send welcome email** (use template in `docs/BETA_ONBOARDING.md`)
2. **Add to tracking spreadsheet**
3. **Schedule 15-min onboarding call** (optional but recommended)
4. **Check in after 3 days**

**Onboarding call agenda:**
1. **Welcome** (2 min) — Thank them, set expectations
2. **Demo** (5 min) — Show them the platform
3. **Setup** (5 min) — Help them create first store
4. **Q&A** (3 min) — Answer questions
5. **Next steps** (1 min) — What to expect

### Day 18-21: Collect Feedback

**Weekly feedback survey:**
1. How easy was it to create your store? (1-10)
2. What do you like most? (open text)
3. What's missing or confusing? (open text)
4. Would you recommend this to a friend? (yes/no)
5. What would make this a 10/10? (open text)

**Track in spreadsheet:**
- Response rate
- Common themes
- Feature requests
- Bugs reported

---

## 📈 Week 4: Scale & Optimize

### Day 22-25: Double Down on What Works

**Analyze your data:**
1. **Which outreach method worked best?** (email, phone, social)
2. **Which business type converted best?** (coffee shops, boutiques, etc.)
3. **Which pitch resonated most?** (ROI, ease of use, competition)
4. **What objections did you hear?** (price, time, need)

**Optimize:**
- Send more of what works
- Stop what doesn't work
- Address common objections in your pitch
- Create case studies from successful customers

### Day 26-28: Prepare for Scale

**If you have 10+ paying customers:**
1. **Create a case study** from your best customer
2. **Ask for referrals** — "Know anyone else who'd benefit?"
3. **Raise prices** — Consider moving to $99/month for new customers
4. **Plan next features** — Based on customer feedback

**If you have <10 paying customers:**
1. **Review your pitch** — What's not working?
2. **Talk to more customers** — Understand their objections
3. **Improve the product** — Fix common issues
4. **Adjust pricing** — Maybe $29/month is better?

1. **Send 10 outreach emails** (personalized)

## 🚨 Common Objections & Responses

### "It's too expensive"
**Response:** "I understand. Most of our beta customers see a 20% increase in foot traffic within the first month. If you get just 2 extra customers per week, that pays for itself. Plus, you're getting 50% off for the first 3 months."

### "I don't have time"
**Response:** "That's exactly why I built this. It takes 60 seconds to set up — less time than posting on Instagram. And once it's set up, it works 24/7 without you doing anything."

### "I already have a website"
**Response:** "That's great! This isn't a replacement — it's a complement. Your website tells people about your business. This lets them experience it. Think of it as a virtual open house that never closes."

### "My customers aren't tech-savvy"
**Response:** "That's the beauty of it — no app downloads, no accounts, no passwords. They just click a link and they're walking through your store. If they can use Facebook, they can use this."

### "I need to think about it"
**Response:** "Totally understand. The beta pricing (50% off) ends this week though. If you're interested, I'd recommend trying it now while it's discounted. You can always cancel if it's not a fit."

## 📊 Success Metrics

**Track these weekly:**
- **Outreach sent:** Target 50/week
- **Response rate:** Target 20% (10 responses)
- **Demo rate:** Target 50% of responses (5 demos)
- **Close rate:** Target 40% of demos (2 signups)
- **MRR:** Target $1,000 by end of month 1

**If you're not hitting these numbers:**
- **Low response rate?** Improve your subject line and opening
- **Low demo rate?** Make your offer more compelling
- **Low close rate?** Address objections better or lower price
- **Low MRR?** Increase prices or close more customers
