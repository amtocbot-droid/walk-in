# Walk In — Monetization Strategy

**Date:** August 2026
**Goal:** Build a profitable, sustainable business around 3D commerce

---

## 🎯 Core Value Proposition

**"Turn your physical store into a 24/7 online showroom in 60 seconds"**

We help brick-and-mortar businesses compete with e-commerce giants by creating immersive 3D shopping experiences that drive foot traffic and online sales.

---

## 💰 Revenue Streams (Ranked by Priority)

### 1. SaaS Subscriptions (Primary Revenue)

**Model:** Monthly/annual recurring revenue from store owners

| Tier | Price | Features | Target Customer |
|------|-------|----------|----------------|
| **Starter** | $29/mo | 1 store, 50 products, basic analytics | Small retail, coffee shops |
| **Professional** | $99/mo | 5 stores, 500 products, AI guide, custom domain | Growing businesses, chains |
| **Enterprise** | $299/mo | Unlimited stores, API access, white-label, priority support | Museums, real estate, large retail |

**Why this works:**
- Predictable recurring revenue
- Low churn if product delivers value
- Scales with customer growth
- Annual billing = 2 months free = better cash flow

**Implementation:**
- Stripe Checkout for payments
- Plan enforcement in owner dashboard
- Usage limits (stores, products, views)
- Upgrade prompts when limits hit

---

### 2. Transaction Fees (High-Margin)

**Model:** Small percentage of sales made through the platform

**Options:**
- **2% transaction fee** on all sales
- **Free tier:** No fees, limited features
- **Pro tier:** Lower fees (1%), more features
- **Enterprise:** Custom rates, volume discounts

**Why this works:**
- Aligns our success with customer success
- High margin (payment processing ~3%)
- Scales with customer GMV
- Customers only pay when they make money

**Implementation:**
- Shopping cart with checkout
- Payment processing (Stripe, PayPal)
- Order management dashboard
- Payout schedule (weekly/monthly)

---

### 3. Retail Media & Advertising (High-Margin)

**Model:** Sponsored placements in 3D stores

**Options:**
- **Sponsored hotspots** — $50-200/month per placement
- **Featured products** — $100-500/month per product
- **Category takeovers** — $500-2000/month per category
- **Branded experiences** — Custom 3D branded spaces

**Why this works:**
- Pure profit (no cost of goods)
- Businesses pay for visibility
- Similar to Amazon Sponsored Products
- High engagement in 3D environment

**Implementation:**
- Retail media dashboard in owner portal
- Impression/click tracking
- Automated billing
- Performance reports

---

## 📊 Pricing Psychology & Packaging

### Freemium Model (Recommended)

**Free Tier:**
- 1 store, 10 products
- Basic 3D viewer
- Community support
- "Powered by Walk In" branding

**Paid Tiers:**
- Remove branding
- More stores/products
- Advanced features
- Priority support

**Why freemium works:**
- Low barrier to entry
- Viral growth through free users
- Natural upgrade path
- Market validation before charging

### Annual Billing Discount

**Monthly:** $99/month
**Annual:** $990/year (2 months free)

**Benefits:**
- Better cash flow
- Lower churn
- Higher LTV
- Customer commitment

### Usage-Based Add-ons

**Base subscription + add-ons:**
- Extra stores: $10/month each
- Extra products: $0.10/month each
- Extra API calls: $0.01/call
- Custom domain: $5/month
- SSL certificate: $10/month

## 🚀 Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Product-Market Fit (Months 1-3)

**Goal:** Validate that businesses will pay for this

**Actions:**
1. **Free beta** with 50 businesses
2. **Customer interviews** to understand pain points
3. **Iterate on product** based on feedback
4. **Measure engagement** (time in store, shares, return visits)

**Success metrics:**
- 50% of beta users active weekly
- 20% willing to pay $29/month
- 10+ case studies/testimonials

### Phase 2: Initial Revenue (Months 4-6)

**Goal:** First $10K MRR

**Actions:**
1. **Launch paid tiers** with Stripe
2. **Content marketing** (blog, SEO, social media)
3. **Direct outreach** to local businesses
4. **Partner with photographers** for 360° content

**Success metrics:**
- 100 paying customers
- $10K MRR
- <5% monthly churn

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 7-12)

**Goal:** $50K MRR and market leadership

**Actions:**
1. **Enterprise sales team** for large accounts
2. **Partner program** for agencies/integrators
3. **API marketplace** for developers
4. **International expansion**

**Success metrics:**
- 500 paying customers
- $50K MRR
- 10 enterprise accounts
- Market leader in 3D commerce

## 💡 Key Success Factors

1. **Product must deliver ROI** — Customers should make more money than they pay us
2. **Low friction onboarding** — Store setup in under 5 minutes
3. **Viral sharing** — Easy for customers to share their stores
4. **Sticky features** — AI guide, analytics, inventory management
5. **Community building** — User groups, case studies, best practices

**Bottom line:** Focus on SaaS subscriptions first, add transaction fees when volume justifies it, build retail media as a high-margin add-on, and use professional services to accelerate enterprise adoption.
